M.Sc. course syllabuses & module leaders

The summaries below follow Surrey’s official 2025/26 catalogue for my programme. Each names the published module leader; this is not a complete list of everyone who contributed to teaching.

EEEM068 · Semester 2 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Applied Machine Learning

Module leader: Dr Muhammad Rana.

Practical deep learning for data from several domains, connecting modern model design to implementation and evaluation.

  • Convolutional and graph-based networks, attention mechanisms and transformers.
  • Learning from limited labels, self-supervision and transfer across domains.
  • Multimodal applications spanning language, vision, audio, healthcare and financial data.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
EEE3043 · Semester 2 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Robotics

Module leader: Professor Simon Hadfield.

Robot systems engineering using ROS, combining software, sensing and decision-making in integrated robotic applications.

  • ROS nodes, robot descriptions, coordinate frames and simulation with Gazebo.
  • Combining sensor data, probabilistic localisation, mapping and SLAM.
  • Motion planning, MoveIt, perception, multiple-agent systems and reinforcement learning.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
EEEM072 · Semester 2 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Research, Professionalism and Innovation

Module leader: Dr Tim Brown.

Research planning and responsible engineering practice, with teamwork and the translation of research into useful innovation.

  • Finding and evaluating literature, referencing and choosing research methods.
  • Inclusive teamwork, design decisions and the societal and environmental effects of technology.
  • Ethics, safety, intellectual property, professional standards and research integrity.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
EEE3032 · Semester 1 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

Module leader: Professor Miroslaw Bober.

Foundations for extracting useful information from images, progressing from pixels and descriptors to recognition, tracking and scene geometry.

  • Filtering, detecting edges, describing image features and classifying visual patterns.
  • Visual retrieval, shape models and tracking with probabilistic filters.
  • Multiple-view geometry and three-dimensional reconstruction, supported by MATLAB exercises.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
ENGM300 · Semester 1 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Medical Robotics

Module leader: Dr Sajeeva Abeywardena.

Applying robot mechanics and control to surgery, rehabilitation and other healthcare settings, including practical and ethical constraints.

  • Robot kinematics, dynamics, planning and control for medical tasks.
  • Sensing and actuation, cooperative and soft robots, and wearable assistance.
  • Clinical integration, commercial and experimental systems, and ethical case studies.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
EEEM071 · Semester 2 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Advanced Topics in Computer Vision and Deep Learning

Module leader: Professor Yi-Zhe Song.

Advanced methods for analysing and generating visual content, linking image-processing foundations with modern deep models.

  • Image transformations, filtering and quality evaluation; neural recognition and segmentation.
  • Generative models, super-resolution and adapting models to new domains.
  • Connecting vision with language and sketches; three-dimensional representations and neural rendering.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
EEEM030 · Semester 1 · 15 UK credits / 7.5 ECTS

Speech & Audio Processing & Recognition

Module leader: Professor Wenwu Wang.

Modelling, analysing and recognising speech and audio, combining signal processing, human perception and statistical learning.

  • Speech production and hearing; spectral, predictive and cepstral analysis.
  • Feature extraction, time alignment, hidden Markov models and probabilistic decoding.
  • Acoustic recording, psychoacoustics, speaker adaptation and robustness to noise.
Official 2025/26 syllabus →
